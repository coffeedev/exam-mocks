CCAO-F Mock Exam (offline package)
==================================

HOW TO USE
1. Unzip this folder anywhere on the computer.
2. Double-click CCAO-F-Mock-Exam.html. It opens in any modern browser
   (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). No installation or internet needed.

WHAT'S INSIDE
- CCAO-F-Mock-Exam.html  The full app: 420 questions, 7 fixed sets, hard mock,
                         instant feedback after every answer in all modes,
                         random 60-question mock, 20-question quick quiz,
                         practice by domain, scoring out of 1,000 (720 to pass).
                         The timer has a Pause button (or press P).
- questions.json         All 420 questions with answers and explanations, for
                         printing or importing into other tools.

NOTES
- Without internet the page uses your system fonts instead of the web fonts.
  Everything else works the same.
- Progress is saved in that browser as you go. Close the page mid-test and
  reopen it to resume where you left off, with the timer paused. A finished
  test keeps its last result, with right and wrong answers, until you press
  Reset (under Saved tests on the start page, or Reset & retake on the
  results page). Saved progress and history stay in that browser on that
  computer and don't move between machines or browsers.
- Scaled scores are an estimate (100 + 900 x correct / questions). Anthropic
  doesn't publish the real conversion.
- All questions are original practice material, not real exam content.
